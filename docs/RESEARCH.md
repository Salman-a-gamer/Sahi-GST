## Claims deliberately excluded

- “All businesses below the e-invoice threshold lack validation.” Existing billing software can offer checks.
- “A valid-looking GSTIN is an active authentic supplier.” Structure cannot establish that.
- “Any mismatched invoice automatically means permanently lost ITC.” Invoice checks are only part of the picture.
- “Green means GST compliant.” It means only the named supported checks passed.
- “Nobody else does AI reconciliation or supplier follow-up.” Competitor pages contradict that.
- “This finds every wrong GST rate.” Product classification and rate applicability are not P0.
- “Three seconds,” “99% accurate,” and “money recovered.” These require actual measurements and stronger evidence.
- “The app works identically on all platforms.” Browser capabilities and tested coverage differ.

## Open decisions before implementation

1. Exact current legal source/applicability for each released rule, including conditional fields, HSN requirements and any checksum algorithm.
2. Runtime vision provider, key, budget, data retention and model quality on small synthetic examples.
3. Actual hosting accounts, service limits and affordable deployment route; no provider selected yet.
4. User's skills and accessible interview participants; proposed persona remains a hypothesis.
5. Whether 20 hours means combined or per person. Baseline assumes combined.
6. Event identity, official full rules and allowed AI/library reuse. Deadline and rubric currently come from the user.
