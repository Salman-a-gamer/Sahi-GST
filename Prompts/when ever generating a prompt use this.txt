when ever writing a prompt use this, 
C - Context  
Set the scene. Tell the AI:
What you do  
What the situation is  
Your goals  
Who the audience is  

R - Role  
Assign the AI a clear identity:
"Act as a senior marketing strategist for SaaS startups"  
"Be a UX designer who specializes in mobile onboarding flows"

I - Instruction  
Explain the task and why it matters:
What you want done  
Why you're doing it  
What success looks like  

S - Specification  
Explain how you want the result delivered:
Format (bullets, slides, paragraphs)  
Length  
Tone and voice  
Must-have elements  

P - Performance  
Set clear quality standards:
Success criteria  
Metrics to hit  
What to avoid  
Industry standards  

E - Example  
Show what good looks like:
Sample content  
Previous success  
Competitor examples
